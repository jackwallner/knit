Ravel is a knitting companion for iPhone and iPad. It helps a knitter keep count of rows, keep their place in a pattern, track their yarn, and knit patterns written to their own measurements. There is no account of any kind, so no demo account is needed. Everything except the optional design list below works with no network.

WHAT A FRESH INSTALL SHOWS
First launch shows three onboarding pages. The last offers the yearly plan's free week ("Try Free for 7 Days", price and renewal terms above the button, Restore, Terms and Privacy below). "Get Started" skips it and opens the free app. Then three tabs: Projects, Patterns and Stash.
1. Projects: tap + to cast on a project. Inside a project, a tap anywhere counts a row and the fabric on screen grows a row with it. A project can hold the knitter's own pattern (a PDF from Files, or photos of a printed pattern), with a row marker over the page. An optional second counter (for example for decreases) sits above the work.
2. Patterns: a library of 32 built-in patterns (hats, socks, mittens, scarves, cowls, shawls, blankets, a pullover, a cardigan, a vest and more), plus seasonal designs. Open one, pick a size or enter measurements, pick a yarn weight or enter a gauge, and the stitch counts and yardage are rewritten for that knitter. "Cast On" starts a guided project that shows one instruction at a time; its menu has "Report a Problem", which opens an email to the developer with the step the knitter is on.
3. Stash: the knitter's yarn (weight, skeins, yardage, fiber, dye lot) and needles. A yarn can be linked to a project.

WHAT IS FREE
Projects, the row counter, the second counter, importing PDFs and photos, and the stash are free with no limits. Patterns marked Free are free: the Garter Scarf and the Diagonal Dishcloth, plus any seasonal design marked Free.

RAVEL PATTERNS (the only purchase)
Unlocks every other pattern. Three ways to buy, all unlocking the same thing:
- com.jackwallner.knit.yearly, auto-renewing, one-week free trial
- com.jackwallner.knit.monthly, auto-renewing, one-week free trial
- com.jackwallner.knit.lifetime, non-consumable, paid once
To reach the paywall: Patterns tab, open any pattern not marked Free (for example Raglan Pullover), tap "Unlock Patterns". The paywall shows each price before the purchase button, with Restore, Terms and Privacy at the bottom. A pattern someone has already cast on stays in their projects if a subscription ends.

WHERE THE PATTERNS COME FROM
Every pattern is written by the app's own sizing code, on the device, from the measurements and gauge the knitter enters. No third-party patterns or content are included.

WHAT THE APP DOWNLOADS
At most once a day the app reads two small JSON data files from its own website (https://jackwallner.github.io/knit/designs.json and /trending.json). designs.json lists seasonal designs as data only (a base design such as a beanie, colors and stripe or chart rows), which the app's built-in sizing code turns into a pattern; trending.json orders the Trending shelf. No executable code is downloaded, and the app works fully without them (it falls back to a list built into the app).

LANGUAGES AND REGIONS
The interface is translated into every language the store listing uses. Generated pattern instructions stay in English, the standard language of written knitting abbreviations. Outside the United States, lengths read in centimeters and meters. Apart from that the app works the same in every region.

IPAD
Ravel runs on iPad in every orientation; wide screens show the project and its pattern side by side.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
RevenueCat manages the purchase state (an anonymous app user id and purchase state only). GitHub Pages hosts the two data files above. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no AI services.

PRIVACY
Projects, counts, PDFs and the stash stay on the device.
