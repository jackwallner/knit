Ravel is a knitting companion that keeps your place. Tap once for every row and the fabric on screen grows with the one in your hands.

FREE, FOR GOOD
• Projects: every piece on your needles, each in its own yarn color
• Row counter: tap anywhere to add a row, one tap to take it back, and the count is saved the instant you tap
• Repeats: set the rows in a repeat and see where you are in it
• Your own patterns: add any PDF, tap a line to mark your place, and count rows without leaving the page
• Stash: yarn by weight and skein count, plus needles and crochet hooks in US and metric sizes
• Works offline, with no account to create

PATTERNS WRITTEN TO YOUR SIZE
Choose a design, enter your measurements and your gauge, and Ravel writes the pattern to fit. Every cast on, increase and decrease is worked out for your numbers, so the stitch counts add up at any size, from a baby hat to a 5X pullover.

Then follow it one row at a time. Each tap shows the next instruction and the stitch count you should have on the needles.

The library has hats, scarves, cowls, blankets, a dishcloth, handwarmers, leg warmers, a shawl and a top-down raglan pullover. The Garter Scarf and the Diagonal Dishcloth are free.

RAVEL PATTERNS
Ravel Patterns unlocks the whole library. Subscribe monthly or yearly, each with a free trial for new subscribers, or pay once for lifetime access.
