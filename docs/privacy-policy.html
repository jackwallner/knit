Privacy Policy

Ravel keeps your knitting on your iPhone. There is no account to create, nothing is sold, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers.

Your projects and stash

Projects, row counts, notes, yarn, needles and hooks are stored on your device. The developer cannot read them.

Your pattern files

PDF patterns you add are copied into the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded.

Library patterns

Patterns from the Ravel library are worked out on your device from the measurements and gauge you enter. Those numbers do not leave your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase records. It does not receive your projects, measurements or files. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

Delete a project or stash item in the app and it is gone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Ravel is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
