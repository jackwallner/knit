Subscription

Ravel Patterns unlocks the pattern library, as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or as a single lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. A subscription renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, and you can cancel any time in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. The lifetime purchase is paid once and does not renew.

Patterns

Patterns are for personal use. Check your gauge with a swatch before you start: the fit depends on it.
