# Privacy Policy: Ravel

> How Ravel handles your data.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/ravel/privacy-policy.html

Effective November 12, 2026

## Privacy Policy

Ravel keeps your knitting on your iPhone. There is no account to create, nothing is sold, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers.

## Your projects and stash

Projects, row counts, notes, yarn, needles and hooks are stored on your device. The developer cannot read them.

## Your pattern files

PDF patterns and photos you add are copied into the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded.

## Library patterns

Patterns from the Ravel library are worked out on your device from the measurements and gauge you enter. Those numbers do not leave your device unless you choose to send them.

If you tap Report a Problem in a pattern, Ravel opens an email in your mail app for you to read, change and send. It holds the pattern's name, the step you are on, your measurements and gauge, the app version, and a code that rebuilds the same pattern. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. Reports are read only to fix the pattern, and you can ask for yours to be deleted at the support address.

## Trending patterns

The Patterns tab downloads a short list of featured patterns from this website. The request carries no account, identifier or knitting data.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase records. It does not receive your projects, measurements or files. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Deleting your data

Delete a project or stash item in the app and it is gone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

Ravel is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
