Counting rows

Open a project and tap the fabric to add a row. The arrow in the corner takes one back. The count is saved the moment you tap.

Reading your own pattern

Open a project, tap the menu and choose Add Pattern to bring in a PDF. Tap a line to mark your place. The mark and the page are there when you come back.

Library patterns

Choose a design, set your size and gauge, and tap Cast On. Each tap moves to the next row, with the stitch count you should have. A pattern you have cast on stays in your projects.

Subscription

Manage or cancel in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open any locked pattern and tap Restore.
