# Popular hand-knit designs, late 2025 to Nov 2026

Research date 2026-11-17. Web only; Ravelry live lists and Reddit were not reachable, so evidence is secondary (trend articles, retailer blogs, Ravelry pattern pages). Treat rankings as informed judgment, not measured sales.

## 2. Motifs and colorways
- Hearts: constant Valentine's seller (links above). Evidence: strong.
- Checkerboard: Katia, Oct 2026 beanie. Strong, and very easy as a 2-color chart.
- Stripes, wide and vertical: Katia, Lantern Moon. Strong.
- Argyle/diamond: KnitPro fall 2026. Medium.
- Cherries and strawberries: many 2026 Ravelry fruit hats, mostly crochet. Medium, and a knit gap.
- Mushrooms/cottagecore: one 2025 hat; weak evidence, keep as a niche.
- Snowflakes and Nordic: perennial winter colorwork (Darling Jadore link).
- Bows: bonnet and bow-tie scarf patterns exist; I found no ranking source. Treat as unconfirmed.
- Pompoms: constant on bonnets, earflap, fruit hats.
- Colorways: tomato red, cherry red with cream, pink, cobalt/cool blue, rust, sage, burgundy, oatmeal and camel neutrals. Offer 2-color palette presets.

## 3. Seasonal angle, Dec 2026 to Feb 2027
- December: gift knitting. Fast projects under a weekend: pompom hats, ear warmers, triangle scarf, chunky cowl, mitts, dishcloth sets. Snowflake, Nordic and red/cream colorwork.
- Late Dec to Jan: cold snaps and resolution knitters. Balaclava, chunky ribbed beanie, earflap hat, thick socks, leg warmers, blanket. Cool blue palette fits Pinterest 2026.
- Early Feb: Valentine's. Heart beanie, heart mitten chart, cherry and strawberry pompom hats, red/pink palettes. Ship by mid January so people can finish.
- Feb: spring-weight pivot, vests, berets, striped tees (Katia says wear-now vests and tees).

## 4. Top 8 to add
Ordered by appeal times ease of engine support.

1. Pompom bonnet with i-cord ties. New shape, huge 2026 fashion pull, cute 5. Rectangle plus back seam or short-row crown; one band and two ties.
2. Heart beanie. Existing beanie with a heart chart repeat sized to stitch count; fits the Feb window.
3. Checkerboard beanie (and checkerboard scarf/cowl). Needs only a 2-color repeat that divides the stitch count; trivial for the engine, strong visual.
4. Fruit hat: strawberry and cherry as pompom or small chart plus a tinted stalk top. Knit version is a market gap; cute 5.
5. Knotting scarf, long and soft (Sophie-style type, generic). Scarf with light-yarn defaults, long length presets, optional fringe or stripes.
6. Small triangle scarf / bandana. Reuse triangle shawl at a compact size, with striped and two-tone versions; Katia and leftover-yarn angle.
7. Striped raglan vest and pullover with wide stripes. Reuse raglan engine; stripe overlay already exists, so add stripe presets and wide bands.
8. Argyle diamond beanie (and vest). Diamond chart is a pure chart overlay with a 2-4 color limit; adds a preppy look no basic beanie has.

Runners-up: beret, pet sweater, mitten with heart chart, chunky ribbed beanie preset in bulky yarn.

## Caveats
- Ravelry "Hot right now" and Etsy bestseller lists were not reachable; several sources are retailer blogs with their own selling motives.
- The Ravelry top-100 is dominated by old free patterns (average age 14 years), so it shows durable categories, not fresh fashion. Fresh signals come from fashion press and Katia, KnitPro, ASOS.
- Bow motifs, mushrooms and TikTok-specific trends had no solid source.
