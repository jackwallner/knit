# @knit.math hands clips: shot list

Five posts, seven shots, about 25 minutes. Hands and yarn only, no face, nobody else in frame. Anyone's hands are fine; captions never say whose.

## Setup (once)

- Phone vertical, rear camera, 1x lens (2x for close-ups is fine, never pinch-zoom). 1080p or 4K, 30 fps.
- Daylight from a window to one side. Lamps off.
- Plain light surface: cream cloth, light wood or white paper. Nothing else on the table.
- One solid, medium-color yarn (rust, mustard, teal). No variegated or very dark yarn, no visible ball band or brand.
- Prop the phone (against a mug or a stack of books, or flat across two books for overhead). No handheld shots.
- Before each shot, press and hold on the yarn until "AE/AF LOCK" shows.
- Keep the bottom quarter of the frame empty: TikTok's caption covers it.
- Two takes of each. Move slowly. Sound does not matter (it gets replaced).

## Shots

| Post | Shot | Frame | What the hands do | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H02 Gauge | A | Overhead on a blocked stockinette swatch, at least 5 in wide, ruler across one row | Pin or needle tip touches each V along 4 inches of ruler, one per half second. Stop on the 4 in mark for 3 seconds | 20-25 s |
| H04 Dropped stitch | B | 45 degrees, close: swatch on the needle with one stitch laddered down 4 rows | Crochet hook goes into the loose loop from the front, catches the next bar, pulls it through. Repeat up the ladder, slip the stitch back on the left needle. Practice once first | 20-30 s |
| H07 Knit vs purl | C | Close, about 6 in from the fabric, knit side up | One fingertip traces a single V, then rests | 8 s |
| | D | Same spot and distance | Flip the swatch over and trace one purl bump | 8 s |
| H10 Count rows | E | Close, stockinette with a column of V's running up the frame | Needle tip climbs one column, pausing on each V, rows 1 to 10 | 15-20 s |
| H13 Three yarns | F | Overhead flat lay: three swatches side by side (fine, worsted, bulky), one ruler across all three with the 4 in mark showing | Slow pan left to right, no hands | 10 s |
| | G | Same flat lay | Pin counts the stitches in 4 in on the bulky swatch | 10-15 s |
