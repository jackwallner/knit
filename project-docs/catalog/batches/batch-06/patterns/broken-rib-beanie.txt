== broken-rib-beanie, Baby, Worsted, 90 yd
14.5 in around, to fit a 16 in head
  About 90 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm 16 in circular needle for the brim
  US 7 · 4.5 mm 16 in circular needle for the body
  Double-pointed needles in the larger size for the crown
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [72]: Cast on 72 stitches with the smaller needle. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x9 [72]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [72]: Change to the larger needle and knit
Body x14 [72]: Knit / *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [64]: *K7, k2tog; repeat from * around

Crown x1 [40]: Knit

Crown x1 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around

Crown x1 [8]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
