== Shrug, S, Worsted, 660 yd
48 in from cuff to cuff, 13 in around the upper arm, with sleeves 14 in long
  About 660 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  US 5 · 3.75 mm needles for the cuffs, and a circular needle in that size for the band
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
First Cuff x1 [42]: With the smaller needle, cast on 42 stitches.
First Cuff x12 [42]: K2, *p2, k2; repeat from * to the end / P2, *k2, p2; repeat from * to the end
Body x1 [58]: Change to the larger needle. Knit, increasing 16 evenly: work (k3, M1) 10 times, then (k2, M1) 6 times
Body x262 [58]: Purl / Knit
Body x1 [58]: Purl
Second Cuff x1 [42]: Change to the smaller needle. Knit, decreasing 16 evenly: work (k2, k2tog) 10 times, then (k1, k2tog) 6 times
Second Cuff x11 [42]: P2, *k2, p2; repeat from * to the end / K2, *p2, k2; repeat from * to the end
Second Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Sleeves x1: Fold the piece in half lengthwise with the right side inside. Starting at one cuff, sew the long edges together for 14 in. Do the same from the other cuff, leaving 20 in open in the middle for the back.
Sleeves x1: Turn the shrug right side out.
Band x1 [164]: With the 32 in circular needle in the smaller size and the right side facing, start at one sleeve seam and pick up and knit 164 stitches evenly around the opening. Join in the round and place a marker.
Band x9 [164]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Band x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends and block the shrug.
