== linen-stitch-washcloth, Small, Worsted, 90 yd
7 in wide, 7 in long
  About 90 yd of worsted weight yarn, cotton for a cloth that scrubs
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Bottom Border x1 [40]: Cast on 40 stitches.
Bottom Border x6 [40]: Knit
Body x58 [40]: K3, *k1, sl1 wyif; repeat from * to the last 5 stitches, k5 / K3, *p1, sl1 wyib; repeat from * to the last 5 stitches, p2, k3
Top Border x6 [40]: Knit
Finishing x1: Bind off loosely. Weave in the ends.
