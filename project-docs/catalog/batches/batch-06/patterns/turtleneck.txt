== Turtleneck Pullover, XS, Worsted, 1060 yd
Finished chest 33 in, to fit a 30 in chest with 3 in of ease. Body 14 in and sleeve 17 in from the underarm
  About 1,060 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm circular needles, 16 in and 32 in
  US 5 · 3.75 mm circular needles, 16 in and 32 in, for the ribbing
  Double-pointed needles in both sizes for the sleeves, or a long circular for magic loop
  4 stitch markers, waste yarn, tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Collar x1 [80]: With the smaller 16 in needle, loosely cast on 80 stitches, so the collar stretches over the head. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Collar x42 [80]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Back Neck x1 [80]: Change to the larger needle. K10 sleeve, place marker, k30 front, place marker, k10 sleeve, place marker, k30 back
Back Neck x1 [80]: Short row: knit to the marker before the front, turn
Back Neck x1 [80]: Make a double stitch, then purl to the marker on the other side of the front, turn
Back Neck x4 [80]: Make a double stitch, knit to the double stitch, knit it as one stitch, k2, turn / Make a double stitch, purl to the double stitch, purl it as one stitch, p2, turn
Back Neck x1 [80]: Make a double stitch, then knit to the start of the round
Back Neck x1 [80]: Knit 1 round, knitting each double stitch as one stitch
Yoke x6 [88 to 104]: *Inc across; repeat from * 3 more times / Knit
Yoke x2 [112]: *Inc across; repeat from * 3 more times. Move the stitches to the 32 in needle / Knit
Yoke x26 [120 to 216]: *Inc across; repeat from * 3 more times / Knit
Yoke x12 [216]: Knit
Divide x1: Slip the 44 stitches of the first sleeve to waste yarn, removing the markers on each side.
Divide x1: Under the arm, cast on 10 stitches with the backward loop method. Knit the 64 front stitches.
Divide x1: Slip the 44 stitches of the second sleeve to waste yarn, removing the markers on each side.
Divide x1 [148]: Under the arm, cast on 10 stitches. Knit the 64 back stitches. Place a marker here for the new start of the round
Body x72 [148]: Knit
Hem x1 [148]: Change to the smaller needle. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Hem x11 [148]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Hem x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
First Sleeve x1 [44]: Return the 44 held sleeve stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the middle of the underarm.
First Sleeve x1 [54]: Pick up and knit 5 stitches, knit the 44 sleeve stitches, then pick up and knit 5 more. Place a marker for the start of the round.
First Sleeve x9 [54]: Knit
First Sleeve x80 [52 to 34]: K1, k2tog, knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit
First Sleeve x1 [34]: Knit
First Sleeve · Cuff x1 [34]: Change to the smaller needles. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
First Sleeve · Cuff x11 [34]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
First Sleeve · Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Second Sleeve x1 [44]: Return the 44 held sleeve stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the middle of the underarm.
Second Sleeve x1 [54]: Pick up and knit 5 stitches, knit the 44 sleeve stitches, then pick up and knit 5 more. Place a marker for the start of the round.
Second Sleeve x9 [54]: Knit
Second Sleeve x80 [52 to 34]: K1, k2tog, knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit
Second Sleeve x1 [34]: Knit
Second Sleeve · Cuff x1 [34]: Change to the smaller needles. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Second Sleeve · Cuff x11 [34]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Second Sleeve · Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends, closing any gaps at the underarms. Block to the finished measurements. Fold the collar down in half to wear it.
