== Hot Water Bottle Cover, Small, Worsted, 100 yd
5.25 in wide and 7.5 in tall below the neck, laid flat, to fit a bottle 5.5 in wide
  About 100 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  A spare needle in the same size for the bind off
  2 stitch markers
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Neck x1 [24]: Cast on 24 stitches and divide them over double-pointed needles, or use a long circular for magic loop. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Neck x24 [24]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Shoulders x1 [24]: K12, place a second marker for the other side, knit to the end
Shoulders x6 [28 to 48]: K1, M1L, knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker, k1, M1L, knit to the last stitch, M1R, k1
Body x39 [48]: Knit
Base x1: Turn the cover inside out. Put the 24 stitches before the second marker on one needle and the 24 after it on another, held parallel.
Base x1: Join the two sides with a three-needle bind off. Turn the cover right side out.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends. Empty the bottle, fold it lengthwise and push it in through the neck, then fill it and fold the ribbed neck down over the stopper.
