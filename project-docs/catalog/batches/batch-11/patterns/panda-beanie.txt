== panda-beanie, Baby, Worsted, 80 yd
14.25 in around, to fit a 16 in head
  Cream, color A: about 70 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Black, color B: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the body, or a long circular for magic loop
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [64]: Cast on 64 stitches in cream with the smaller needle. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x9 [64]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [64]: Change to the larger needle and knit
Body x15 [64]: Knit
Crown x1 [56]: *K6, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [56]: Knit
Crown x1 [48]: *K5, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [48]: Knit
Crown x1 [40]: *K4, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [40]: Knit
Crown x1 [32]: *K3, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [32]: Knit
Crown x1 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [16]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [8]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 8 stitches and pull tight.
First Ear x1 [10]: Cast on 10 stitches in black.
First Ear x4 [10]: Knit / Purl
First Ear x1 [8]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
First Ear x1 [8]: Purl
First Ear x1 [6]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
First Ear x1 [6]: Purl
First Ear x1 [4]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
First Ear x1 [4]: Purl
First Ear x1: Bind off.
First Ear x1 [20]: With black, pick up and knit 20 stitches around the sides and top of the ear, leaving the cast-on edge free.
First Ear x1 [20]: Knit
First Ear x1: Bind off knitwise, leaving a tail to sew the ear on with.
Second Ear x1 [10]: Cast on 10 stitches in black.
Second Ear x4 [10]: Knit / Purl
Second Ear x1 [8]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
Second Ear x1 [8]: Purl
Second Ear x1 [6]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
Second Ear x1 [6]: Purl
Second Ear x1 [4]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
Second Ear x1 [4]: Purl
Second Ear x1: Bind off.
Second Ear x1 [20]: With black, pick up and knit 20 stitches around the sides and top of the ear, leaving the cast-on edge free.
Second Ear x1 [20]: Knit
Second Ear x1: Bind off knitwise, leaving a tail to sew the ear on with.
Finishing x1: With black and a tapestry needle, work the chart in duplicate stitch over the cream fabric: 7 rounds high and 11 stitches wide. It covers stitches 12 to 22 of the round, counted from the marker, and starts on round 15 above the cast on.
Finishing x1: Chart round 1, the bottom one, left to right: skip 5, 1 black, skip 5.
Finishing x1: Chart round 2, left to right: skip 4, 3 black, skip 4.
Finishing x1: Chart round 3, left to right: skip 1, 3 black, skip 3, 3 black, skip 1.
Finishing x1: Chart round 4, left to right: 5 black, skip 1, 5 black.
Finishing x1: Chart round 5, left to right: 2 black, skip 1, 2 black, skip 1, 2 black, skip 1, 2 black.
Finishing x1: Chart round 6, left to right: 5 black, skip 1, 5 black.
Finishing x1: Chart round 7, left to right: 4 black, skip 3, 4 black.
Finishing x1: Sew the cast-on edge of each ear to the crown, right side out, one on each side of the top and about 3.25 in apart. Line them up with the face: the front of the hat is the half that begins at the start of the round, so center the pair over it. Weave in the ends.
