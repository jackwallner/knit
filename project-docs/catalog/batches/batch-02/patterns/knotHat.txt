== Baby Knot Hat, Newborn, Worsted, 110 yd
12.5 in around, to fit a 13.5 in head, 5.75 in from the brim to the knot
  About 110 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [56]: Cast on 56 stitches with the smaller needles. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x6 [56]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [56]: Change to the larger needles and knit
Body x20 [56]: Knit
Crown x2 [48]: *K5, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [40]: *K4, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [32]: *K3, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x1 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x24 [24]: Knit
Crown x1: Bind off loosely. Weave in the ends.
Finishing x1: Tie the tube in a loose overhand knot, snug against the top of the hat.
