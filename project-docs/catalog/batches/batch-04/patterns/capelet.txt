== Capelet, Small, Worsted, 310 yd
18.75 in around the collar, 45.25 in around the hem, 10 in long, for a 32 in chest
  About 310 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm 16 in circular needle for the collar
  US 7 · 4.5 mm 32 in circular needle for when the stitches crowd the short one
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Collar x1 [84]: Cast on 84 stitches loosely. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Collar x15 [84]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around

Yoke x5 [104]: Knit
Yoke x1 [124]: Work (k6, M1) 4 times, then (k5, M1) 16 times
Yoke x5 [124]: Knit

Yoke x5 [164]: Knit
Yoke x1 [184]: Work (k9, M1) 4 times, then (k8, M1) 16 times
Yoke x5 [184]: Knit
Yoke x1 [204]: Work (k10, M1) 4 times, then (k9, M1) 16 times
Yoke x8 [204]: Knit
Hem x6 [204]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Hem x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends and block the capelet over a towel shaped like your shoulders.
