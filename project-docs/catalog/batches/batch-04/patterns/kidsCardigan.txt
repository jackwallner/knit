== Kids' Cardigan, 2T, Worsted, 440 yd
Finished chest 24 in with the bands buttoned, to fit a 21 in chest with 3 in of ease. Body 8.5 in and sleeve 8.5 in from the underarm
  About 440 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm circular needle, 32 in or longer, worked flat
  US 5 · 3.75 mm circular needle of the same length, for the ribbing and bands
  Double-pointed needles in both sizes for the sleeves, or a long circular for magic loop
  5 buttons, about 0.5 in across
  4 stitch markers, waste yarn, tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
