== Mug Cozy, Small, Worsted, 40 yd
3 in tall, 10.25 in long, to wrap a 9.5 in mug
  About 40 yd of worsted weight yarn, cotton or wool
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  2 buttons, about 0.5 in across
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Band x1 [13]: Cast on 13 stitches.
Band x65 [13]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the last stitch, k1
Band x1 [13]: K1, p1, yo, k2tog, work in seed stitch to the last 4 stitches, yo, k2tog, p1, k1
Band x2 [13]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the last stitch, k1
Band x1: Bind off in pattern.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends. Wrap the band around the mug with the handle between the two buttonholes, the buttonhole end over the cast-on end, and sew a button to the cast-on end under each buttonhole.
