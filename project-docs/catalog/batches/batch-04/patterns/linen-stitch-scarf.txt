== linen-stitch-scarf, Child, Worsted, 450 yd
5 in wide, 48 in long
  About 450 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Scarf x1 [30]: Cast on 30 stitches.
Scarf x490 [30]: *K1, sl1 wyif; repeat from * to the last 2 stitches, k2 / *P1, sl1 wyib; repeat from * to the last 2 stitches, p2
Finishing x1: Bind off loosely in pattern. Weave in the ends.
