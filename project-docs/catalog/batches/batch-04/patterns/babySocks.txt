== Baby Socks, Newborn, Fingering, 90 yd
3.5 in around, to fit a 4 in foot. The foot is 3.25 in from the back of the heel to the tip of the toe, and the leg 2 in from the cuff to the heel
  About 90 yd of fingering weight yarn, wool with a little nylon wears longest
  US 1 · 2.25 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  2 stitch markers
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 32 stitches and 44 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Sock 1 · Cuff x1 [28]: Cast on 28 stitches. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Sock 1 · Cuff x7 [28]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Sock 1 · Leg x15 [28]: Knit
Sock 1 · Heel Flap x1: The heel flap is worked back and forth over the first 14 stitches of the round. Leave the other 14 on their needle for the instep.
Sock 1 · Heel Flap x14 [14]: *Slip 1 purlwise with the yarn in back, k1; repeat from * across the heel, turn / Slip 1 purlwise with the yarn in front, purl to the end of the heel, turn

Sock 1 · Heel Turn x1 [8]: Slip 1 purlwise, purl to 1 stitch before the gap, p2tog, p1, turn
Sock 1 · Gusset x1: With the right side facing, knit across the 8 heel stitches, then pick up and knit 8 stitches along the edge of the flap, one in each slipped stitch and one in the corner.
Sock 1 · Gusset x1 [38]: Place a marker for the new start of the round. Knit the 14 instep stitches and place a marker. Pick up and knit 8 stitches along the other edge of the flap the same way, then knit to the start of the round.
Sock 1 · Gusset x10 [36 to 28]: Knit the instep to the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit
Sock 1 · Foot x13 [28]: Knit
Sock 1 · Toe x7 [24 to 12]: K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit
Sock 1 · Toe x1 [8]: K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
Sock 1 · Toe x1: Cut the yarn, leaving a 12 in tail. Put the 4 instep stitches on one needle and the 4 sole stitches on another, and graft them together with Kitchener stitch. Weave in the ends.
