== Boot Cuffs, Small, Worsted, 140 yd
10.75 in around, to fit a 12 in calf with the rib stretched a little, 6 in tall
  About 140 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Leg x1 [48]: Cast on 48 stitches loosely. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Leg x16 [48]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Cuff x17 [48]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around / *P1, k1; repeat from * around
Edge x3 [48]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Edge x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends. Make a second cuff the same way. Wear the rib inside the boot, with the seed stitch above it or folded down over the top.
