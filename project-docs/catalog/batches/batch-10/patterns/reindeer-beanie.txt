== reindeer-beanie, Baby, Worsted, 100 yd
14.25 in around, to fit a 16 in head
  Navy, color A: about 70 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Brown, color B: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Red, color C: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Cream, color D: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the body, or a long circular for magic loop
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [64]: Cast on 64 stitches in navy with the smaller needle. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x9 [64]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [64]: Change to the larger needle and knit
Body x15 [64]: Knit
Crown x1 [56]: *K6, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [56]: Knit
Crown x1 [48]: *K5, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [48]: Knit
Crown x1 [40]: *K4, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [40]: Knit
Crown x1 [32]: *K3, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [32]: Knit
Crown x1 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [16]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [8]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Finishing x1: With brown, red and cream and a tapestry needle, work the chart in duplicate stitch over the navy fabric: 10 rounds high and 11 stitches wide. It covers stitches 12 to 22 of the round, counted from the marker, and starts on round 13 above the cast on.
Finishing x1: Chart round 1, the bottom one, left to right: skip 4, 1 brown, 1 red, 1 brown, skip 4.
Finishing x1: Chart round 2, left to right: skip 3, 5 brown, skip 3.
Finishing x1: Chart round 3, left to right: skip 2, 7 brown, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Chart round 4, left to right: skip 2, 1 brown, skip 1, 3 brown, skip 1, 1 brown, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Chart round 5, left to right: skip 1, 9 brown, skip 1.
Finishing x1: Chart round 6, left to right: skip 2, 7 brown, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Chart round 7, left to right: skip 3, 5 brown, skip 3.
Finishing x1: Chart round 8, left to right: skip 2, 2 cream, skip 3, 2 cream, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Chart round 9, left to right: skip 1, 1 cream, skip 1, 1 cream, skip 3, 1 cream, skip 1, 1 cream, skip 1.
Finishing x1: Chart round 10, left to right: 1 cream, skip 1, 1 cream, skip 5, 1 cream, skip 1, 1 cream.
Finishing x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 8 stitches and pull tight. Weave in the ends.
