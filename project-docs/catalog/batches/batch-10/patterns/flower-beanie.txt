== flower-beanie, Baby, Worsted, 90 yd
14.25 in around, to fit a 16 in head
  Cream, color A: about 50 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Pink, color B: about 20 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Mustard, color C: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Green, color D: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the body, or a long circular for magic loop
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [64]: Cast on 64 stitches in pink with the smaller needle. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x9 [64]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [64]: With cream, change to the larger needle and knit
Body x15 [64]: Knit
Crown x1 [56]: *K6, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [56]: Knit
Crown x1 [48]: *K5, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [48]: Knit
Crown x1 [40]: *K4, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [40]: Knit
Crown x1 [32]: *K3, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [32]: Knit
Crown x1 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [16]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [8]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Finishing x1: With green, pink and mustard and a tapestry needle, work the chart in duplicate stitch over the cream stripe: 8 rounds high and 7 stitches wide. It covers stitches 14 to 20 of the round, counted from the marker, with 4 rounds of cream under it.
Finishing x1: Chart round 1, the bottom one, left to right: skip 2, 3 green, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Chart round 2, left to right: skip 1, 5 green, skip 1.
Finishing x1: Chart round 3, left to right: skip 3, 1 green, skip 3.
Finishing x1: Chart round 4, left to right: skip 2, 3 pink, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Chart round 5, left to right: skip 1, 2 pink, 1 mustard, 2 pink, skip 1.
Finishing x1: Chart round 6, left to right: 2 pink, 3 mustard, 2 pink.
Finishing x1: Chart round 7, left to right: skip 1, 2 pink, 1 mustard, 2 pink, skip 1.
Finishing x1: Chart round 8, left to right: skip 2, 3 pink, skip 2.
Finishing x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 8 stitches and pull tight. Weave in the ends.
