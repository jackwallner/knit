== Tube Socks, Child, DK, 180 yd
5.5 in around unstretched, to fit a 6 in foot, and 14.25 in from the cuff to the toe
  About 180 yd of DK weight yarn
  US 6 · 4 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  2 stitch markers
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 22 stitches and 28 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Cuff x1 [30]: Cast on 30 stitches loosely and divide them over double-pointed needles, or use a long circular for magic loop. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Cuff x12 [30]: *K3, p3; repeat from * around
Spiral Rib x78 [30]: *K3, p3; repeat from * around / *K3, p3; repeat from * around / *K3, p3; repeat from * around / *K3, p3; repeat from * around. Remove the marker, k1 and place the marker again, so the rib moves one stitch to the left
Toe x1 [30]: Knit 15 stitches for the top of the foot and place a marker. Knit the last 15 for the sole
Toe x6 [26 to 18]: *K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker; repeat from * once more / Knit
Toe x2 [14 to 10]: *K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker; repeat from * once more
Toe x1: Put the 5 stitches of the top on one needle and the 5 of the sole on another, removing the markers. Cut the yarn, leaving a 12 in tail, and graft the toe shut with Kitchener stitch.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends and make a second sock the same way. The socks have no heel: the spiral rib settles around the heel as they are worn.
