== Tank Top, XS, DK, 600 yd
Finished chest 32 in, to fit a 30 in chest with 2 in of ease. 13 in from the hem to the underarm and 7.5 in from the underarm to the shoulder
  About 600 yd of DK weight yarn
  US 6 · 4 mm 24 in circular needle
  US 4 · 3.5 mm circular needles, 16 in and 24 in, for the ribbing
  Stitch marker, waste yarn, tapestry needle
  Gauge: 22 stitches and 28 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
