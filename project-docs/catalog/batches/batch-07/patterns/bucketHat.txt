== Bucket Hat, Child, Worsted, 160 yd
19.5 in around the band, to fit a 20 in head, with a 2.25 in brim
  About 160 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop, and a 16 in circular needle
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Crown x1 [8]: Leaving a 6 in tail, cast on 8 stitches and divide them over double-pointed needles, or use a long circular for magic loop. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Crown x1 [8]: Knit
Crown x2 [16]: *K1, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [24]: *K2, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [32]: *K3, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [40]: *K4, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [48]: *K5, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [56]: *K6, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [64]: *K7, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [72]: *K8, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [80]: *K9, M1L; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 16 in needle / Knit
Crown x2 [88]: *K10, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x1 [88]: Purl. The ridge turns the crown down into the sides
Sides x20 [88]: Knit
Sides x1 [88]: Purl. The ridge turns the sides out into the brim
Brim x1 [102]: Knit, increasing 14 evenly: work (k7, M1) 4 times, then (k6, M1) 10 times
Brim x3 [102]: Knit
Brim x1 [118]: Knit, increasing 16 evenly: work (k7, M1) 6 times, then (k6, M1) 10 times
Brim x3 [118]: Knit
Brim x1 [132]: Knit, increasing 14 evenly: work (k9, M1) 6 times, then (k8, M1) 8 times
Brim x4 [132]: Knit
Edge x3 [132]: Purl / Knit
Edge x1: Bind off loosely purlwise.
Finishing x1: Thread the cast-on tail through the first 8 stitches, pull it snug to close the center of the crown and weave in the ends. Steam the brim flat.
