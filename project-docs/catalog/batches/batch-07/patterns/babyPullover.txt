== Baby Pullover, 3 Months, DK, 300 yd
Finished chest 19 in, to fit a 16 in chest with 3 in of ease. Body 6 in and sleeve 6 in from the underarm
  About 300 yd of DK weight yarn
  US 6 · 4 mm circular needles, 12 in and 16 in, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 4 · 3.5 mm needle of the same kind, for the ribbing and bands
  Double-pointed needles in both sizes for the sleeves, or a long circular for magic loop
  2 buttons, about 0.5 in across
  4 stitch markers, waste yarn, tapestry needle
  Gauge: 22 stitches and 28 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Neckband x1 [64]: With the smaller needle, cast on 64 stitches. The neck is worked back and forth at first, open where the back meets a sleeve.
Neckband x6 [64]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the end
Yoke x1 [64]: Change to the larger needle. K8 sleeve, place marker, k24 front, place marker, k8 sleeve, place marker, k24 back, turn
Yoke x1 [64]: Purl to the end, turn
Yoke x4 [72 to 80]: *Inc across; repeat from * 2 more times. K1, M1L, knit to the last stitch, M1R, k1, turn / Purl to the end, turn
Yoke x2 [84]: Inc across the sleeve. Knit the front. Inc across the sleeve. Knit to the end, turn / Purl to the end, turn. Join in the round to close the opening, and place a marker for the start of the round
Yoke x2 [92]: *Inc across; repeat from * 3 more times. Move the stitches to the longer needle / Knit
Yoke x10 [100 to 132]: *Inc across; repeat from * 3 more times / Knit
Yoke x2 [136]: Inc across the sleeve. Knit the front. Inc across the sleeve. Knit the back / Knit
Yoke x4 [144 to 152]: *Inc across; repeat from * 3 more times / Knit
Divide x1: Slip the 32 stitches of the first sleeve to waste yarn, removing the markers on each side.
Divide x1: Under the arm, cast on 8 stitches with the backward loop method. Knit the 44 front stitches.
Divide x1: Slip the 32 stitches of the second sleeve to waste yarn, removing the markers on each side.
Divide x1 [104]: Under the arm, cast on 8 stitches. Knit the 44 back stitches. Place a marker here for the new start of the round
Body x34 [104]: Knit
Hem x1 [104]: Change to the smaller needle. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Hem x7 [104]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Hem x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
First Sleeve x1 [32]: Return the 32 held sleeve stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the middle of the underarm.
First Sleeve x1 [40]: Pick up and knit 4 stitches, knit the 32 sleeve stitches, then pick up and knit 4 more. Place a marker for the start of the round.
First Sleeve x11 [40]: Knit
First Sleeve x20 [38 to 32]: K1, k2tog, knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit
First Sleeve x3 [32]: Knit
First Sleeve · Cuff x1 [32]: Change to the smaller needles. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
First Sleeve · Cuff x7 [32]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
First Sleeve · Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Second Sleeve x1 [32]: Return the 32 held sleeve stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the middle of the underarm.
Second Sleeve x1 [40]: Pick up and knit 4 stitches, knit the 32 sleeve stitches, then pick up and knit 4 more. Place a marker for the start of the round.
Second Sleeve x11 [40]: Knit
Second Sleeve x20 [38 to 32]: K1, k2tog, knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit
Second Sleeve x3 [32]: Knit
Second Sleeve · Cuff x1 [32]: Change to the smaller needles. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Second Sleeve · Cuff x7 [32]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Second Sleeve · Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Button Band x1 [11]: With the smaller needle and the right side facing, pick up and knit 11 stitches down the back edge of the opening, from the top of the neckband to the base.
Button Band x4 [11]: P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end / K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Button Band x1: Bind off in pattern.
Buttonhole Band x1 [11]: With the smaller needle and the right side facing, pick up and knit 11 stitches up the sleeve edge of the opening, from the base to the top of the neckband.
Buttonhole Band x1 [11]: P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end / K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Buttonhole Band x1 [11]: Work 1 in rib, then work (k2tog, yo, work 5 in rib) once. K2tog, yo, then work the last 1 in rib
Buttonhole Band x1 [11]: P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end, working each yarn over as a stitch
Buttonhole Band x1 [11]: K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end / P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end
Buttonhole Band x1: Bind off in pattern.
Finishing x1: Lap the buttonhole band over the button band at the base of the opening and sew both ends down. Sew the 2 buttons to the button band, opposite the buttonholes. Weave in the ends, closing any gaps at the underarms.
