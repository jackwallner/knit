== Tote Bag, Small, Worsted, 430 yd
A base 12 in wide and 4 in deep, 11 in tall, with handles 14 in long between the ends sewn on, stretching to about 16 in
  About 430 yd of worsted weight yarn, cotton or linen so the bag holds its shape
  US 7 · 4.5 mm 24 in circular needle
  4 stitch markers, one in a different color
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Base x1 [54]: Cast on 54 stitches.
Base x36 [54]: Knit
Body x1 [144]: Do not turn. Place a marker, pick up and knit 18 stitches along the side of the base, one in each garter ridge, place a marker, pick up and knit 54 along the cast-on edge, place a marker, pick up and knit 18 along the other side, and place the marker in a different color for the start of the round. Join in the round. A marker now sits at each corner.
Body x2 [144]: *Slip 1 purlwise with the yarn in back, knit to the next marker; repeat from * around. The slipped stitches crease the corners / Knit
Body x58 [144]: *Slip 1 purlwise with the yarn in back, knit to the next marker; repeat from * around / Knit
Band x10 [144]: Purl / Knit
Band x1: Bind off loosely purlwise.
First Handle x1 [6]: Cast on 6 stitches.
First Handle x162 [6]: Knit
First Handle x1: Bind off, leaving a long tail to sew with.
Second Handle x1 [6]: Cast on 6 stitches.
Second Handle x162 [6]: Knit
Second Handle x1: Bind off, leaving a long tail to sew with.
Finishing x1: Flatten the bag so the creases sit at its sides. Lay the last 2 in of each end of one handle flat on the outside of the front, from the top edge down over the band, each end 2.75 in from a side. Sew around the edges of each end, through both layers. Sew the other handle to the back the same way. Weave in the ends.
