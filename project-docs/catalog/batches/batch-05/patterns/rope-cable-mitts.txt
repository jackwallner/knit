Mitt 1 · Top x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Mitt 1 · Thumb x1 [12]: Pick up the 5 stitches below the waste yarn and the 5 above it, then take out the waste yarn. Join the yarn and pick up 1 more stitch at each corner. Place a marker.

Mitt 1 · Thumb x5 [10]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Mitt 1 · Thumb x1: Bind off loosely in pattern. Weave in the ends, closing any gap at the corners of the thumb.
