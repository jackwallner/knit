== Mini Stocking Ornament, Small, Worsted, 30 yd
2.25 in wide laid flat. The foot is 2.5 in from the back of the heel to the tip of the toe, and the leg 2 in from the cuff to the heel
  About 30 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  2 stitch markers
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Cuff x1 [20]: Cast on 20 stitches. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Cuff x4 [20]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Leg x8 [20]: Knit
Heel Flap x1: The heel flap is worked back and forth over the first 10 stitches of the round. Leave the other 10 on their needle for the instep.
Heel Flap x10 [10]: *Slip 1 purlwise with the yarn in back, k1; repeat from * across the heel, turn / Slip 1 purlwise with the yarn in front, purl to the end of the heel, turn
Heel Turn x1 [9]: Slip 1 purlwise, k4, ssk, k1, turn
Heel Turn x1 [8]: Slip 1 purlwise, p1, p2tog, p1, turn
Heel Turn x1 [7]: Slip 1 purlwise, knit to 1 stitch before the gap, ssk, k1, turn
Heel Turn x1 [6]: Slip 1 purlwise, purl to 1 stitch before the gap, p2tog, p1, turn
Gusset x1: With the right side facing, knit across the 6 heel stitches, then pick up and knit 6 stitches along the edge of the flap, one in each slipped stitch and one in the corner.
Gusset x1 [28]: Place a marker for the new start of the round. Knit the 10 instep stitches and place a marker. Pick up and knit 6 stitches along the other edge of the flap the same way, then knit to the start of the round.
Gusset x3 [26 to 22]: Knit the instep to the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
Gusset x1 [22]: Knit
Gusset x2 [20]: Knit the instep to the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit
Toe x5 [16 to 8]: K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit
Toe x1: Cut the yarn, leaving a 12 in tail. Put the 4 instep stitches on one needle and the 4 sole stitches on another, and graft them together with Kitchener stitch. Weave in the ends.
Finishing x1: Cut three strands of yarn, each 8 in long. Knot them together at one end, braid them, and knot the other end. Fold the braid in half and sew both knots inside the cuff at the back of the leg, above the heel, so the loop stands above the edge. Weave in the ends.
