== honeycomb-beanie, Baby, Worsted, 80 yd
14.5 in around, to fit a 16 in head
  About 80 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the body, or a long circular for magic loop
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [68]: Cast on 68 stitches with the smaller needle. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x9 [68]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [68]: Change to the larger needle and knit
Body x16 [68]: *K1, sl1 wyif; repeat from * around / Knit / *Sl1 wyif, k1; repeat from * around / Knit
Body x1 [64]: Knit, decreasing 4 evenly: work (k15, k2tog) 4 times
Crown x1 [56]: *K6, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [56]: Knit
Crown x1 [48]: *K5, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [48]: Knit
Crown x1 [40]: *K4, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [40]: Knit
Crown x1 [32]: *K3, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [32]: Knit
Crown x1 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [16]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [8]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Finishing x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 8 stitches and pull tight. Weave in the ends.
