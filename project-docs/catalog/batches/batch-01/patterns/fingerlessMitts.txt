== Fingerless Mitts, Child, Worsted, 80 yd
5.25 in around, to fit a 6 in hand, 6 in from the cuff to the knuckles
  About 80 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the ribbing, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the hand, or a long circular
  2 stitch markers, waste yarn
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Mitt 1 · Cuff x1 [24]: Cast on 24 stitches with the smaller needles. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Mitt 1 · Cuff x12 [24]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Mitt 1 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: Change to the larger needles and knit
Mitt 1 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: Knit
Mitt 1 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: K1, place a thumb marker, knit to the end. The stitch before the thumb marker is the gusset
Mitt 1 · Thumb Gusset x9 [26 to 30]: M1L, knit to the thumb marker, M1R, slip the marker, knit to the end / Knit / Knit
Mitt 1 · Thumb Gusset x2 [30]: Knit
Mitt 1 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: Slip the 7 gusset stitches to waste yarn and take out the thumb marker. Cast on 1 stitch over the gap with the backward loop method, then knit to the end
Mitt 1 · Hand x6 [24]: Knit
Mitt 1 · Top x1 [24]: Change to the smaller needles and work *k2, p2; repeat from * around
Mitt 1 · Top x2 [24]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Mitt 1 · Top x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Mitt 1 · Thumb x1 [10]: Return the 7 held thumb stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the gap and pick up and knit 3 stitches: one in the corner, one in the stitch cast on over the gap, and one in the other corner. Place a marker for the start of the round.
Mitt 1 · Thumb x1 [8]: Knit to the last 4 stitches, k2tog, k2tog
Mitt 1 · Thumb x3 [8]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Mitt 1 · Thumb x1: Bind off loosely in pattern. Weave in the ends, closing any gap at the base of the thumb.
Mitt 2 · Cuff x1 [24]: Cast on 24 stitches with the smaller needles. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Mitt 2 · Cuff x12 [24]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Mitt 2 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: Change to the larger needles and knit
Mitt 2 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: Knit
Mitt 2 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: K1, place a thumb marker, knit to the end. The stitch before the thumb marker is the gusset
Mitt 2 · Thumb Gusset x9 [26 to 30]: M1L, knit to the thumb marker, M1R, slip the marker, knit to the end / Knit / Knit
Mitt 2 · Thumb Gusset x2 [30]: Knit
Mitt 2 · Thumb Gusset x1 [24]: Slip the 7 gusset stitches to waste yarn and take out the thumb marker. Cast on 1 stitch over the gap with the backward loop method, then knit to the end
Mitt 2 · Hand x6 [24]: Knit
