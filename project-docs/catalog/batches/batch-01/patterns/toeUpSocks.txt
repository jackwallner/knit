== Toe-Up Socks, Child, Fingering, 260 yd
6 in around, to fit a 6.5 in foot. The foot is 6.75 in from the back of the heel to the tip of the toe, and the leg 4 in from the heel to the cuff
  About 260 yd of fingering weight yarn, wool with a little nylon wears longest
  US 1 · 2.25 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  A needle 2 sizes larger for the bind off
  2 stitch markers
  Smooth waste yarn in a contrasting color
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 32 stitches and 44 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Sock 1 · Toe x1 [12]: Cast on 12 stitches with Judy's magic cast-on, 6 on each of two needles. The first needle holds the top of the foot, the second the sole. Work in the round from here, each round starting at the top of the foot.
Sock 1 · Toe x1 [12]: Knit the top of the foot, place a marker, then knit the sole
Sock 1 · Toe x4 [16 to 28]: K1, M1L, knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker, k1, M1L, knit to the last stitch, M1R, k1
Sock 1 · Toe x1 [28]: Knit
Sock 1 · Toe x9 [32 to 48]: K1, M1L, knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker, k1, M1L, knit to the last stitch, M1R, k1 / Knit
Sock 1 · Foot x43 [48]: Knit
Sock 1 · Foot x1 [48]: Knit to the marker. With a smooth waste yarn in a contrasting color, knit the 24 sole stitches, slip them back to the left needle and knit them again with the sock yarn
Sock 1 · Leg x31 [48]: Knit
Sock 1 · Cuff x13 [48]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Sock 1 · Cuff x1: Bind off very loosely in rib, with a needle 2 sizes larger, so the cuff stretches over the heel.
Sock 1 · Heel x1: Pick out the waste yarn and catch the live stitches on two needles: 24 from the foot below the gap and 24 from the leg above it. If the leg side shows one short, pick up the half stitch at its end.
Sock 1 · Heel x1 [50]: Join the yarn at the right edge of the foot needle. Knit the 24 foot stitches, pick up and knit 1 stitch in the corner of the gap and place a marker. Knit the 24 leg stitches, pick up and knit 1 stitch in the other corner, and place a marker for the start of the round.
Sock 1 · Heel x1 [48]: Knit to 2 stitches before the marker, k2tog, slip the marker, knit to the last 2 stitches, k2tog
Sock 1 · Heel x11 [44 to 24]: K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit
Sock 1 · Heel x3 [20 to 12]: K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
Sock 1 · Heel x1: Cut the yarn, leaving a 12 in tail. Put the 6 stitches on each side of the marker on their own needle and graft them together with Kitchener stitch. Close any gap left at the corners with the tail, and weave in the ends.
Sock 2 · Toe x1 [12]: Cast on 12 stitches with Judy's magic cast-on, 6 on each of two needles. The first needle holds the top of the foot, the second the sole. Work in the round from here, each round starting at the top of the foot.
Sock 2 · Toe x1 [12]: Knit the top of the foot, place a marker, then knit the sole
Sock 2 · Toe x4 [16 to 28]: K1, M1L, knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker, k1, M1L, knit to the last stitch, M1R, k1
Sock 2 · Toe x1 [28]: Knit
Sock 2 · Toe x9 [32 to 48]: K1, M1L, knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker, k1, M1L, knit to the last stitch, M1R, k1 / Knit
Sock 2 · Foot x43 [48]: Knit
Sock 2 · Foot x1 [48]: Knit to the marker. With a smooth waste yarn in a contrasting color, knit the 24 sole stitches, slip them back to the left needle and knit them again with the sock yarn
Sock 2 · Leg x31 [48]: Knit
Sock 2 · Cuff x13 [48]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Sock 2 · Cuff x1: Bind off very loosely in rib, with a needle 2 sizes larger, so the cuff stretches over the heel.
Sock 2 · Heel x1: Pick out the waste yarn and catch the live stitches on two needles: 24 from the foot below the gap and 24 from the leg above it. If the leg side shows one short, pick up the half stitch at its end.
Sock 2 · Heel x1 [50]: Join the yarn at the right edge of the foot needle. Knit the 24 foot stitches, pick up and knit 1 stitch in the corner of the gap and place a marker. Knit the 24 leg stitches, pick up and knit 1 stitch in the other corner, and place a marker for the start of the round.

Sock 2 · Heel x3 [20 to 12]: K1, ssk, knit to 3 stitches before the marker, k2tog, k1, slip the marker, k1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1
