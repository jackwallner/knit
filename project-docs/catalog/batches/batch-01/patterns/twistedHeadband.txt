== Twisted Headband, Child, Worsted, 90 yd
4 in wide and 18 in long before the twist, to fit a 20 in head
  About 90 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Band x1 [18]: Cast on 18 stitches, leaving a tail of 12 in for sewing.
Band x162 [18]: Knit
Band x1: Bind off loosely, leaving a tail of 12 in.
Finishing x1: Lay the band flat with the two ends side by side. Fold the left end in half lengthwise, bringing its edges together.
Finishing x1: Fold the right end in half the same way, hooking it through the fold of the left end, so the two folded ends interlock.
Finishing x1: Bring the four layers of cast-on and bound-off edges together and sew through all of them with one tail. Turn the band so the seam is inside, and the twist sits at the front. Weave in the ends.
