ok   Poncho: 6 sizes x 8 weights

== Poncho, Child, Worsted, 430 yd
Two panels, each 11 in by 22 in, sewn into a poncho that hangs about 15.5 in from the neck to the point
  About 430 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm circular needle, 32 in or longer, worked flat
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Panel 1 x1 [50]: Cast on 50 stitches.
Panel 1 x6 [50]: Knit
Panel 1 x120 [50]: Knit / K3, purl to the last 3 stitches, k3
Panel 1 x6 [50]: Knit
Panel 1 x1: Bind off loosely. Weave in the ends.
Panel 2 x1 [50]: Cast on 50 stitches.
Panel 2 x6 [50]: Knit
Panel 2 x120 [50]: Knit / K3, purl to the last 3 stitches, k3
Panel 2 x6 [50]: Knit
Panel 2 x1: Bind off loosely. Weave in the ends.
Finishing x1: Block both panels to 11 in by 22 in. Hold each with its right side facing you and its cast-on edge at the bottom: its left side is the long edge on your left.
Finishing x1: Sew the cast-on edge of Panel 2 along the top half of Panel 1's left side, from the bound-off corner down to the middle.
Finishing x1: Sew the cast-on edge of Panel 1 along the top half of Panel 2's left side the same way. Each panel's end is now sewn to the side of the other, and the two free halves of the sides frame the neck. Weave in the ends.
