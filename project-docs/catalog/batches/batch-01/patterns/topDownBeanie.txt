== Top-Down Beanie, Baby, Worsted, 80 yd
14.25 in around, to fit a 16 in head, 6 in from the top to the brim
  About 80 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Crown x1 [8]: Cast on 8 stitches, leaving a tail of 8 in, and divide them over double-pointed needles or a long circular for magic loop. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Crown x1 [16]: *K1, M1L; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [24]: *K2, M1L; repeat from * around
Crown x2 [32]: *K3, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [40]: *K4, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [48]: *K5, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x2 [56]: *K6, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Crown x1 [64]: *K7, M1L; repeat from * around
Body x16 [64]: Knit
Brim x1 [64]: Change to the smaller needles. *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Brim x8 [64]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Brim x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Finishing x1: Thread the cast-on tail through the 8 cast-on stitches and pull tight to close the top. Weave in the ends.
