== Baby Cardigan, 3 Months, DK, 330 yd
Finished chest 19 in with the bands buttoned, to fit a 16 in chest with 3 in of ease. Body 6 in and sleeve 6 in from the underarm
  About 330 yd of DK weight yarn
  US 6 · 4 mm circular needle, 32 in or longer, worked flat
  US 4 · 3.5 mm circular needle of the same length, for the ribbing and bands
  Double-pointed needles in both sizes for the sleeves, or a long circular for magic loop
  5 buttons, about 0.5 in across
  4 stitch markers, waste yarn, tapestry needle
  Gauge: 22 stitches and 28 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Neckband x1 [58]: With the smaller needle, cast on 58 stitches.
Neckband x8 [58]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the end
Back Neck x1 [58]: Change to the larger needle. K9 front, place marker, k8 sleeve, place marker, k24 back, place marker, k8 sleeve, place marker, k9 front
Back Neck x1 [58]: Purl
Back Neck x1 [58]: Short row: knit to the marker before the last front, turn
Back Neck x1 [58]: Make a double stitch, then purl to the marker before the last front, turn
Back Neck x4 [58]: Make a double stitch, knit to the double stitch, knit it as one stitch, k3, turn / Make a double stitch, purl to the double stitch, purl it as one stitch, p3, turn
Back Neck x1 [58]: Make a double stitch, then knit to the end, knitting the double stitch you pass as one stitch
Back Neck x1 [58]: Purl to the end, purling the last double stitch as one stitch
Yoke x4 [66 to 74]: Knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker. *Inc across; repeat from * 2 more times. K1, M1L, knit to the end / Purl
Yoke x2 [78]: Knit the front. Inc across the sleeve. Knit the back. Inc across the sleeve. Knit the front / Purl
Yoke x12 [86 to 126]: Knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker. *Inc across; repeat from * 2 more times. K1, M1L, knit to the end / Purl
Yoke x2 [130]: Knit the front. Inc across the sleeve. Knit the back. Inc across the sleeve. Knit the front / Purl
Yoke x4 [138 to 146]: Knit to 1 stitch before the marker, M1R, k1, slip the marker. *Inc across; repeat from * 2 more times. K1, M1L, knit to the end / Purl
Yoke x2 [146]: Knit / Purl
Divide x1: Knit the 19 front stitches. Slip the 32 stitches of the first sleeve to waste yarn, removing the markers on each side.
Divide x1: Under the arm, cast on 8 stitches with the backward loop method. Knit the 44 back stitches.
Divide x1: Slip the 32 stitches of the second sleeve to waste yarn, removing the markers on each side.
Divide x1 [98]: Under the arm, cast on 8 stitches. Knit the 19 front stitches
Body x34 [98]: Purl / Knit
Hem x1 [98]: Change to the smaller needle. *K1, p1; repeat from * to the end
Hem x7 [98]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the end
Hem x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
First Sleeve x1 [32]: Return the 32 held sleeve stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the middle of the underarm.
First Sleeve x1 [40]: Pick up and knit 4 stitches, knit the 32 sleeve stitches, then pick up and knit 4 more. Place a marker for the start of the round.
First Sleeve x11 [40]: Knit
First Sleeve x20 [38 to 32]: K1, k2tog, knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit
First Sleeve x3 [32]: Knit
First Sleeve · Cuff x1 [32]: Change to the smaller needles. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
First Sleeve · Cuff x7 [32]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
First Sleeve · Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Second Sleeve x1 [32]: Return the 32 held sleeve stitches to the larger needles. Join new yarn at the middle of the underarm.
Second Sleeve x1 [40]: Pick up and knit 4 stitches, knit the 32 sleeve stitches, then pick up and knit 4 more. Place a marker for the start of the round.
Second Sleeve x11 [40]: Knit
Second Sleeve x20 [38 to 32]: K1, k2tog, knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit / Knit
Second Sleeve x3 [32]: Knit
Second Sleeve · Cuff x1 [32]: Change to the smaller needles. *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Second Sleeve · Cuff x7 [32]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Second Sleeve · Cuff x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Button Band x1 [65]: With the smaller needle and the right side facing, pick up and knit 65 stitches along the left front edge, from the top of the neckband down to the hem. To button it the other way, work this band on the right front and the buttonhole band on the left.
Button Band x8 [65]: P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end / K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Button Band x1: Bind off in pattern.
Buttonhole Band x1 [65]: With the smaller needle and the right side facing, pick up and knit 65 stitches along the right front edge, from the hem up to the top of the neckband.
Buttonhole Band x3 [65]: P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end / K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Buttonhole Band x1 [65]: Work 2 in rib, then work (k2tog, yo, work 13 in rib) 3 times, then (k2tog, yo, work 12 in rib) once. K2tog, yo, then work the last 2 in rib
Buttonhole Band x1 [65]: P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end, working each yarn over as a stitch
Buttonhole Band x3 [65]: K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end / P1, *k1, p1; repeat from * to the end
Buttonhole Band x1: Bind off in pattern.
Finishing x1: Sew the 5 buttons to the button band, opposite the buttonholes. Weave in the ends, closing any gaps at the underarms. Block to the finished measurements.
