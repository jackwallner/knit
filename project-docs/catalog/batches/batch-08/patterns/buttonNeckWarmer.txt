== Button Neck Warmer, Small, Bulky, 80 yd
6 in tall and 16.75 in long, buttoning into a ring 14.25 in around
  About 80 yd of bulky weight yarn, soft wool or a wool blend
  US 10.5 · 6.5 mm needles
  3 buttons, about 0.75 in across
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 14 stitches and 20 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Band x1 [21]: Cast on 21 stitches. This edge is the button end.
Band x86 [21]: K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Buttonholes x1 [15]: Work 2 stitches in pattern, bind off the next 2 stitches, work until there are 5 stitches after the gap, bind off the next 2 stitches, work until there are 5 stitches after the gap, bind off the next 2 stitches and work the last 3 stitches in pattern
Buttonholes x1 [21]: Work in pattern, casting on 2 stitches over each gap with the backward loop method
Buttonholes x4 [21]: K1, *p1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Buttonholes x1: Bind off loosely in pattern.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends. Wrap the band around the neck with the buttonhole end on top, mark where each buttonhole falls on the cast-on end, and sew a button at each mark.
