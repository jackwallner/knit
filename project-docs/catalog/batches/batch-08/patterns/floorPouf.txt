== Floor Pouf, Small, Super Bulky, 530 yd
16.25 in across and 12 in tall, filled
  About 530 yd of super bulky weight yarn, a hard-wearing wool or cotton blend
  US 13 · 9 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop, and 16 in, 24 in and 32 in circular needles
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Polyester fiberfill or soft fabric scraps, enough to fill 16.25 in across and 12 in tall firmly
  Gauge: 10 stitches and 14 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Top x1 [8]: Leaving a 6 in tail, cast on 8 stitches and divide them over double-pointed needles, or use a long circular for magic loop. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Top x1 [8]: Knit
Top x2 [16]: *K1, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [24]: *K2, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [32]: *K3, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit

Top x2 [56]: *K6, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [64]: *K7, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [72]: *K8, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [80]: *K9, M1L; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 24 in needle / Knit
Top x2 [88]: *K10, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [96]: *K11, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [104]: *K12, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [112]: *K13, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Top x2 [120]: *K14, M1L; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 32 in needle / Knit
Top x2 [128]: *K15, M1L; repeat from * around / Knit
Sides x1 [128]: Purl. The ridge turns the top down into the sides
Sides x40 [128]: *K3, p1; repeat from * around
Sides x1 [128]: Purl. The ridge turns the sides under into the bottom
Bottom x1: The bottom narrows as you go: move the stitches to shorter circular needles, then to double-pointed needles, as they stop reaching around.
Bottom x2 [120]: *K14, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [112]: *K13, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [104]: *K12, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [96]: *K11, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [88]: *K10, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [80]: *K9, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [72]: *K8, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [64]: *K7, k2tog; repeat from * around. Stuff the pouf firmly through the opening now, and keep adding stuffing as it closes / Knit
Bottom x2 [56]: *K6, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [48]: *K5, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [40]: *K4, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [32]: *K3, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [24]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x2 [16]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Bottom x1 [8]: K2tog around
Finishing x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 8 stitches, push in a last handful of stuffing, pull it snug and fasten off. Close the center of the top with the cast-on tail the same way, and weave in the ends.
