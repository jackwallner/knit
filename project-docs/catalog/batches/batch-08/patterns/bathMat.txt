== Bath Mat, Small, Super Bulky, 290 yd
16.75 in by 24 in
  About 290 yd of super bulky weight yarn, cotton, which soaks up water and washes hot
  US 13 · 9 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop, and 16 in, 24 in, 32 in and 40 in circular needles as the mat grows
  4 stitch markers, one a different color
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 10 stitches and 14 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Middle x1 [40]: Leaving a 14.5 in tail, cast on 40 stitches. Join in the round without twisting and place markers to divide the round into 4 sections of 18, 2, 18 and 2 stitches, a different color marker at the start of the round.
Body x2 [48]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 16 in needle / Purl
Body x6 [56 to 72]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around / Purl
Body x2 [80]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 24 in needle / Purl
Body x8 [88 to 112]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around / Purl
Body x2 [120]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 32 in needle / Purl
Body x10 [128 to 160]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around / Purl
Body x2 [168]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around. Move the stitches to the 40 in needle / Purl
Body x8 [176 to 200]: *M1L, knit to the next marker, M1R, slip the marker; repeat from * around / Purl
Body x1 [200]: Knit
Body x1: Bind off loosely purlwise.
Finishing x1: Flatten the cast-on ring into a straight line down the middle of the mat, with the two short sections at its ends, and sew its two halves together with the cast-on tail. Weave in the ends and block the mat flat.
