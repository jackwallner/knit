== Baby Bib, Newborn, Worsted, 70 yd
6 in wide and 6 in from the hem to the neck, with straps that button behind the neck
  About 70 yd of worsted weight yarn, cotton, which soaks up spills and washes well
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  A button about 0.5 in across
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Bottom x1 [21]: Cast on 21 stitches.
Bottom x6 [23 to 27]: K1, kfb, knit to the last 2 stitches, kfb, k1 / Knit
Body x48 [27]: Knit
Body x1 [9]: With the right side facing, k9, bind off the next 9 stitches for the neck, and knit to the end. Work the 9 stitches after the gap first, leaving the others on the needle
First Strap x4 [9 to 7]: Knit / K1, k2tog, knit to the end
First Strap x32 [7]: Knit
First Strap x1 [7]: K2, yo, k2tog, knit to the end. This is the buttonhole
First Strap x3 [7]: Knit
First Strap x1: Bind off.
Second Strap x1 [9]: With the wrong side facing, join the yarn at the neck edge of the other 9 stitches.
Second Strap x4 [9 to 7]: Knit / Knit to the last 3 stitches, ssk, k1
Second Strap x36 [7]: Knit
Second Strap x1: Bind off.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends. Sew the button to the end of the second strap, so the straps overlap behind the neck and the button meets the buttonhole.
