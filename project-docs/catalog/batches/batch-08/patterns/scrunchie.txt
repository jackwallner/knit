== Scrunchie, Small, DK, 40 yd
A strip 3 in wide and 12 in long, gathered into a ring about 4 in across
  About 40 yd of DK weight yarn, cotton, silk or a soft wool
  US 6 · 4 mm needles
  A hair elastic about 2 in across
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 22 stitches and 28 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Strip x1 [17]: Cast on 17 stitches, leaving a 12 in tail.
Strip x84 [17]: Knit / Purl
Strip x1: Bind off, leaving a 24 in tail to sew with.
Finishing x1: Lay the hair elastic on the wrong side of the strip. Fold the strip lengthwise around it, right side out, and sew the long edges together with the long tail, pushing the strip along the elastic as you go so it gathers. Sew the cast-on edge to the bound-off edge with the other tail, and weave in the ends.
