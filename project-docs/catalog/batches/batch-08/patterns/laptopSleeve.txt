== Laptop Sleeve, Small, Worsted, 190 yd
10.75 in wide and 7.5 in deep inside, with a 2.5 in flap
  About 190 yd of worsted weight yarn, a sturdy wool or wool blend
  US 7 · 4.5 mm 16 in circular needle, and a spare needle in the same size for the bind off
  A button about 0.75 in across
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Flap x1 [48]: Cast on 48 stitches. This edge is the tip of the flap.
Flap x4 [48]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the end / *P1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Flap x1 [45]: Work 22 stitches in pattern, bind off the next 3 stitches for the buttonhole and work in pattern to the end
Flap x1 [48]: Work in pattern to the gap, cast on 3 stitches over it with the backward loop method and work in pattern to the end
Flap x10 [48]: *K1, p1; repeat from * to the end / *P1, k1; repeat from * to the end
Body x1 [96]: With the right side facing, work across the flap in pattern, then cast on 48 stitches for the front. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker. The flap stitches are now the back
Body x50 [96]: *P1, k1; repeat from * around / *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Bottom x1: Turn the sleeve inside out. Put the 48 back stitches on one needle and the 48 front stitches on another, held parallel.
Bottom x1: Join them with a three-needle bind off, and turn the sleeve right side out.
Finishing x1: Weave in the ends. Fold the flap over the front, mark where the buttonhole falls and sew the button there.
