== Cat Ear Hat, Baby, Worsted, 100 yd
14.25 in around, to fit a 16 in head, 6.75 in from the brim to the top edge
  About 100 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular
  A spare needle of the same size for the bind off
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [64]: Cast on 64 stitches with the smaller needles. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x8 [64]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x1 [64]: Change to the larger needles and knit
Body x31 [64]: Knit
Top x1: Turn the hat inside out. Put the first 32 stitches on one needle and the last 32 on another, so the start of the round sits at one side, and join them with a three-needle bind off.
Finishing x1: Turn the hat right side out and weave in the ends. For rounder ears, wrap yarn tightly around each top corner 1 in down and knot it inside.
