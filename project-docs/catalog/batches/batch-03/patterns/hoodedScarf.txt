== Hooded Scarf, Child, Worsted, 560 yd
A hood 11 in from the top to the neck, with ends 6.75 in wide hanging 18 in below it
  About 560 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Scarf x1 [30]: Cast on 30 stitches.
Scarf x162 [30]: Knit
Increase x14 [32 to 44]: K1, kfb, knit to the last 2 stitches, kfb, k1 / Knit
Hood x170 [44]: Knit
Decrease x14 [42 to 30]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit
Scarf x162 [30]: Knit
Scarf x1: Bind off loosely.
Finishing x1: Fold the piece in half across the middle of the hood. Sew the two halves together along one long edge, from the fold down 11 in: that seam is the back of the hood, and the open edge frames the face. Weave in the ends.
