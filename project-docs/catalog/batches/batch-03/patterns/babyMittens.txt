== Baby Mittens, Newborn, Worsted, 50 yd
4.5 in around, to fit a 4.25 in hand, 4.5 in from the cuff to the tip
  About 50 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the cuff, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the hand, or a long circular
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Mitten 1 · Cuff x1 [20]: Cast on 20 stitches with the smaller needles. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Mitten 1 · Cuff x10 [20]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Mitten 1 · Hand x1 [20]: Change to the larger needles and knit
Mitten 1 · Hand x12 [20]: Knit
Mitten 1 · Top x2 [15]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Mitten 1 · Top x1 [10]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Mitten 1 · Top x1 [5]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Mitten 1 · Top x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 5 stitches and pull tight. Weave in the ends.
Mitten 2 · Cuff x1 [20]: Cast on 20 stitches with the smaller needles. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Mitten 2 · Cuff x10 [20]: *K1, p1; repeat from * around
Mitten 2 · Hand x1 [20]: Change to the larger needles and knit
Mitten 2 · Hand x12 [20]: Knit
Mitten 2 · Top x2 [15]: *K2, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit
Mitten 2 · Top x1 [10]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Mitten 2 · Top x1 [5]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Mitten 2 · Top x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 5 stitches and pull tight. Weave in the ends.
