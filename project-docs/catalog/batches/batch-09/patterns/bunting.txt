ok   Bunting: 5 sizes x 8 weights

== Bunting, Small, Worsted, 140 yd
8 flags, each 4.75 in wide and 5 in long, on a cord with ties at both ends
  About 140 yd of worsted weight yarn, cotton or wool, in one color or several
  US 7 · 4.5 mm needles
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rows over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Flag x1 [21]: Cast on 21 stitches.
Flag x2 [21]: Knit
Flag x1 [21]: K1, *yo, k2tog; repeat from * to the end. These eyelets carry the cord
Flag x3 [21]: Knit
Flag x36 [19 to 3]: K1, ssk, knit to the last 3 stitches, k2tog, k1 / Knit / Knit / Knit
Flag x1 [1]: Slip 1, k2tog, pass the slipped stitch over
Flag x1: Cut the yarn and pull it through the last stitch. Weave in the ends.
Flag x1: Make 7 more flags the same way, 8 in all.
Cord x1: Cut two strands of yarn, each 234 in long. Knot them together at both ends, anchor one end and twist the other until the strands kink back on themselves.
Cord x1: Fold the twisted strands in half and let them twist around each other into a cord about 78 in long. Knot the loose end.
Finishing x1: Block the flags flat. Leaving 15 in of cord free at each end for tying, weave the cord in and out through the eyelets of each flag in turn, about 1 in apart.
