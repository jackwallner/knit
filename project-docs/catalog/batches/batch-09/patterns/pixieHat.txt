== Pixie Hat, Newborn, Worsted, 90 yd
12.5 in around and 10.5 in from the brim to the tip, to fit a 13.5 in head
  About 90 yd of worsted weight yarn, soft and machine washable
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles, or a long circular for magic loop
  4 stitch markers, one a different color
  A piece of card for the tassel
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [56]: Cast on 56 stitches and divide them over double-pointed needles, or use a long circular for magic loop. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.
Brim x6 [56]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x17 [56]: Knit
Body x1 [56]: *K14, place a marker; repeat from * 2 more times, and knit to the end. The start of the round marker is the fourth
Point x39 [52 to 4]: *Knit to 2 stitches before the next marker, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit / Knit
Finishing x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 4 stitches, pull it snug and fasten off. Make a tassel about 2 in long and sew it to the tip. Weave in the ends.

== Pixie Hat, Child, Worsted, 180 yd

Brim x8 [80]: *K2, p2; repeat from * around
Body x25 [80]: Knit
Body x1 [80]: *K20, place a marker; repeat from * 2 more times, and knit to the end. The start of the round marker is the fourth
Point x57 [76 to 4]: *Knit to 2 stitches before the next marker, k2tog; repeat from * around / Knit / Knit
Finishing x1: Cut the yarn, thread it through the last 4 stitches, pull it snug and fasten off. Make a tassel about 3 in long and sew it to the tip. Weave in the ends.
