== strawberry-beanie, Baby, Worsted, 80 yd
14.25 in around, to fit a 16 in head
  Cream, color A: about 50 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Red, color B: about 20 yd of worsted weight yarn
  Green, color C: about 10 yd of worsted weight yarn
  US 5 · 3.75 mm double-pointed needles for the brim, or a long circular for magic loop
  US 7 · 4.5 mm double-pointed needles for the body, or a long circular for magic loop
  Stitch marker
  Tapestry needle
  Gauge: 18 stitches and 24 rounds over 4 in of stockinette, after blocking. Use the needle that gives you this gauge
Brim x1 [64]: Cast on 64 stitches in red with the smaller needle. Join in the round without twisting and place a marker.

Body x1 [64]: With cream, change to the larger needle and knit
Body x15 [64]: Knit
Crown x1 [56]: *K6, k2tog; repeat from * around

Crown x1 [16]: *K1, k2tog; repeat from * around
Crown x1 [8]: *K2tog; repeat from * around
Finishing x1: With red and green and a tapestry needle, work the chart in duplicate stitch over the cream stripe: 10 rounds high and 9 stitches wide. It covers stitches 13 to 21 of the round, counted from the marker, with 3 rounds of cream under it.
